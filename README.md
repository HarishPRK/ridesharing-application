# Community RideShare – Decentralized Rides for Local Communities
Application aiming to decentralise ridesharing

Uber and Lyft run their businesses on a centralized model, allowing for high service commissions, very unpredictable earnings for drivers, and nonsensical peak pricing for passengers. They also do not always serve the needs of smaller, local communities. Through this Capstone project, we will solve these problems by creating a peer-to-peer ride-sharing platform that enables riders and drivers with equal bargaining power. The system being community-oriented allows users to set their prices and conduct business transactions, in effect introducing fairness and a localized approach to ridesharing.

Benefits
•	Affordable Rides: Users set their own fares, making rides more budget friendly.
•	Community-Oriented: Strengthens local connections by encouraging ridesharing within neighborhoods.
•	Transparency & Fairness: Eliminates intermediaries, ensuring fair pricing.
•	Flexible Ride Options: Drivers and riders can negotiate ride details directly.
•	Enhanced Security: A structured rating and review system helps build trust.
